Entry for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun — a complete story in exactly six words.

## The story

> **He finally arrived home. Nobody remembered.**

Six words, one whole life in them: a long absence, a homecoming, and the turn — nobody remembered him. Why he left, how long he was gone, what he expected to find: the reader fills all of that in.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as the bounty requires (UTF-8, 1–60 characters, exactly six words — asserted in code before writing).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
